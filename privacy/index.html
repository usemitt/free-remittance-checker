Privacy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This page covers the free remittance checker from TickApply at https://usemitt.github.io/free-remittance-checker/, and the guide pages on this site.

Your files stay in your browser

  • The checker runs entirely in your web browser. Your remittance file and the invoice numbers you paste are read and matched on your own device.
  • Your remittance and invoice data are not uploaded to any server. The checker page also sets a Content Security Policy that blocks it from making network requests.
  • Your remittance data is not stored by us. When you close or reload the page, it's gone. The results CSV is only saved if you choose to download it, and then only to your own computer.
  • The checker does not connect to QuickBooks Online or any other accounting system, and it never reads from or writes to your books.

No account, no email

You don't need an account to use the checker, and it doesn't ask for your name. The waitlist box shown after results is not connected yet: anything typed there is not sent or saved. If that changes, this page will be updated first to say what is collected and where it's kept.

No analytics or cookies

The checker counts runs only in your open browser tab; that count is not sent anywhere. The site does not set cookies or use third-party analytics or ad trackers.

Hosting

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic request information (such as your IP address and browser type) when you load a page. That is handled under GitHub's privacy statement. Your remittance files are not part of those requests.

Not affiliated with Intuit

QuickBooks is a trademark of Intuit Inc. TickApply and this checker are not affiliated with or endorsed by Intuit.

Questions

You can open an issue on the site's public GitHub repository, github.com/usemitt/free-remittance-checker. Issues are public, so please don't post remittance files, invoice data or other private information there.

Changes

If the checker's behavior changes, this page will be updated and the date above changed.
