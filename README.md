# TickApply — free remittance checker

**Live:** https://usemitt.github.io/free-remittance-checker/

Check a customer's remittance advice (CSV or Excel) against your open invoice numbers, free and in your
browser. No account, no upload: files never leave your browser, and the checker does not connect to
QuickBooks or write anything to your books.

## How-to guides (QuickBooks Online)

- [Apply one payment to multiple invoices](https://usemitt.github.io/free-remittance-checker/how-to/apply-one-payment-multiple-invoices-qbo/)
- [Turn a remittance advice into a Receive payment](https://usemitt.github.io/free-remittance-checker/how-to/remittance-advice-to-receive-payment-qbo/)
- [Apply an unapplied payment to an invoice](https://usemitt.github.io/free-remittance-checker/how-to/apply-unapplied-payment-to-invoice-qbo/)
